# Mlangeni Group

**Building useful software for Malawi and the world.**

## Company profile

Mlangeni Group is a software development company founded and owned by **Precious Lyson**. Established as a sole proprietorship, the company exists to create software and digital applications that solve meaningful problems and make a positive difference in Malawi and around the world.

The company works across **web, mobile, and desktop applications**. Its interests span the full journey from identifying a need and shaping an idea to designing, building, and improving a practical digital product. Mlangeni Group aims to make technology useful, accessible, and relevant to the people and communities it serves.

## Our purpose

### Mission

To develop useful, dependable software and digital applications that address real needs and contribute to positive change in Malawi and beyond.

### Vision

To grow into a trusted software company whose products help people, businesses, and communities make the most of technology—locally and globally.

### Values

- **Purposeful innovation:** We look for meaningful problems to solve, rather than building technology for its own sake.
- **Local understanding:** We value the needs, opportunities, and perspectives of people in Malawi.
- **Practicality:** We aim to make products useful, understandable, and fit for their intended purpose.
- **Quality and care:** We strive to build thoughtful software and keep improving it.
- **Learning and growth:** We welcome new ideas, technologies, and feedback as part of making better products.
- **Positive impact:** We want our work to contribute constructively to the communities and users it reaches.

## What we do

Mlangeni Group develops and explores digital products across several platforms:

- **Web applications** for services, information, and online workflows.
- **Mobile applications** designed to put useful tools in people's hands.
- **Desktop applications** for focused tasks and computer-based workflows.
- **Software frameworks and developer tools** that can help people create and extend software.

The specific solution depends on the problem. Our aim is to understand the need first, then build an application suited to its users and purpose.

## How we approach projects

Our approach is guided by a few straightforward principles:

1. **Understand the need.** Start with the problem, intended users, and desired outcome.
2. **Shape a practical solution.** Define what the product should do and which platform best fits its purpose.
3. **Build with care.** Develop the application with attention to usability, reliability, and maintainability.
4. **Learn and improve.** Use feedback and experience to refine the product over time.

## Who we aim to serve

Mlangeni Group is interested in creating software for people and organisations with problems that digital tools can help solve. This includes opportunities in Malawi as well as ideas with relevance to users elsewhere in the world.

## Looking ahead

Mlangeni Group is committed to developing its products, exploring new ideas, and contributing to a stronger digital future. The long-term goal is to create software that is not only technically useful, but also makes a meaningful difference to the people who use it.
